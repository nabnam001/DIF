25 seasons · Aarhus

The lecture series archive.

DIF Aarhus's lecture series has invited Danish and Israeli scholars, journalists, artists, rabbis and practitioners to Aarhus since the 1999/2000 season. A look at the archive.

2024–2025 · current

The season is under way. Write to us if you'd like to be added to the invitation list, or see events.

2023–2024

The season included talks on Israel's internal democratic debate, Jewish life in Aarhus, and a curated literary evening.

2022–2023

Lectures on contemporary antisemitism, the post-Trump Middle Eastern landscape, and an Israeli film evening.

2021–2022 · re-opening after Covid

The season opened with a tasting of Israeli wines on 14 September 2021. Member price 350 DKK, non-member 450 DKK — including coffee, tea and cake.

2020–2021 · Covid pause

The association ran adapted online events and a few smaller in-person lectures when the pandemic permitted.

2019–2020

3 Dec 2019: 40 years of kosher supply in Denmark — David M. Gerschwald. 23 Oct 2019: Keren Hayesod and The Dead Sea Revival Project — Noam Bedein.

2018–2019

10 Mar 2019: "From Peace Instrument to Sustaining Hostility" — Shaun Sacks (NGO Monitor) — in cooperation with MIFF Denmark. Lecture in English.

2017–2018

26 Oct 2017: Circumcision & freedom of conscience — Chief Rabbi Jair Melchior (the Jewish Community in Denmark).

2016–2017

Jul 2016: "From Rockets to Rainbows" — Noam Bedein on Sderot and life under rocket fire from Gaza.

2015–2016

The season's six lectures covered Israeli social debate, Jewish history in Scandinavia, and an evening on Hebrew poetry.

2014–2015

Lectures on Operation Protective Edge (Gaza 2014), antisemitism in Europe, and an Israeli literature evening.

2013–2014

Lectures on Danish-Jewish history, Israeli democracy, and travel evenings from the Holy Land.

2012–2013

The season included talks on Israeli innovation (start-up nation), the kibbutz movement, and an evening on Holocaust memorials.

2011–2012

Lectures on the Middle East after the Arab Spring, and on Theodor Herzl and the founding of Zionism.

2010–2011

The season included a lecture on Israel's War of Independence and the Danish volunteers (Søren Espersen).

2009–2010

Lectures on antisemitism in modern Europe, and on Bent Melchior's life story.

2008–2009

The season marked Israel's 60th anniversary with multiple lectures on the country's history and present.

2007–2008

Lectures on Jewish humour (Arne Melchior), klezmer music, and travel evenings from the Negev and Galilee.

2006–2007

Lectures on Iran and Israel, Jewish culture in Copenhagen, and an evening on Etgar Keret.

2005–2006

The season featured lectures on the Dead Sea, kibbutz life, and the Israeli political landscape.

2004–2005 · Gabrielle Bense, chair

Lectures on Jewish philosophy (Maimonides/Rambam), and on Carmi Gillon, Israel's ambassador to Denmark 2001–2003.

2003–2004

Lectures on Theodor Herzl, and on the 2003 Israeli elections.

2002–2003

The season's lectures covered the Second Intifada, Jewish identity in Denmark, and an evening on Anne Frank.

2001–2002 · 9/11 and aftermath

Lectures on the new security landscape in the Middle East after 11 September 2001.

2000–2001

Lectures on the Camp David negotiations, and on Jewish heritage in Denmark.

1999–2000 · first documented season

The lecture series in its current form is established as a backbone of the association's work.

Want to know more about a particular season or lecture? Write to dif_webmaster@yahoo.dk. The association's archive contains titles, speakers and a brief description of every lecture since 1976.
