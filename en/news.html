Holidays

Coming Jewish and national holidays

Lag Baomer is a minor non-biblical holiday that has become very popular in modern Israel and which strengthens the bonds to the heroic and legendary Jewish past.

Yom Yerushalayim — Jerusalem Day — celebrates the Israeli army's liberation of the Old City, the Temple Mount, the Western Wall and the rest of Jerusalem in June 1967 after 19 years of Jordanian occupation. The city was reunited and free access to the holy sites was restored to all faiths.

Antisemitism

Antisemitism in Denmark

Mistrust and prejudice against Jews in Denmark is not confined to extremist groups. Several studies have shown this. DIF Aarhus has worked against antisemitism and anti-Israeli currents through education and dialogue since the association's founding in 1976.
