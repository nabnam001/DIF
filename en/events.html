Events & activities.

DIF Aarhus runs a yearly lecture series of 6–7 talks from September to April, plus extra lectures, wine tastings, concerts, film evenings and trips to Israel. The programme is announced here and members receive invitations directly.

Time

Season

The lecture series runs from September to April. The annual general meeting is usually held in September or October.

Place

Folkeoplysningens Hus

Vester Allé 8, 8000 Aarhus C — Café Nicolai or room 2.2. Other addresses are shared on registration.

Price

Lecture series

350 DKK member · 450 DKK non-member
Includes coffee/tea and cake. Discounts for students and young people.

A glimpse of past events

Selected from the archive

DIF has hosted hundreds of lectures and events since 1976. Here are a few from recent years.

3Dec 2019

40 years of kosher supply in Denmark

David M. Gerschwald on his family's history and a working life in kosher supply — with samples and a small market of kosher goods from Israel.

Lecture
23Oct 2019

Keren Hayesod & The Dead Sea Revival Project

Noam Bedein on the United Israel Appeal and an NGO working to save the Dead Sea — with photo journalism and short films.

Lecture
10Mar 2019

From Peace Instrument to Sustaining Hostility

Shaun Sacks of NGO Monitor — on the Oslo Peace Process, civil society and NGO funding. In English, in cooperation with MIFF Denmark.

Lecture
26Oct 2017

Circumcision & freedom of conscience

Chief Rabbi Jair Melchior on circumcision in Jewish religion and the right to practise it in Danish society.

Lecture
—Jul 2016

From Rockets to Rainbows — Sderot

Noam Bedein on the city of Sderot, life under rocket fire from Gaza, and how civil society finds courage and hope.
