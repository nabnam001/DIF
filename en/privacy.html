Privacy policy

How the Danish-Israeli Association (DIF) Aarhus handles your personal data. The association is the data controller and complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act.

Data controller

Dansk-Israelsk Forening (DIF), Aarhus
CVR number: 40376445
Contact: dif_webmaster@yahoo.dk

What information do we process?

DIF Aarhus only processes information necessary to run the association's activities. This typically means:

  • Member information: name, postal address, email address, phone number (if shared), year of joining.
  • Membership payments: date and amount. The bank transaction itself is handled by the financial institution.
  • Lecture series sign-ups: name and contact details.
  • Contact enquiries: the content of emails you send us.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (health, religious or political beliefs, ethnicity, sexuality, etc.).

Legal basis and purpose

Processing takes place to:

  • fulfil the association's obligations to members (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) — contract),
  • send invitations and newsletters about the association's activities (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) — you can withdraw consent at any time),
  • comply with legal requirements, e.g. the Danish Bookkeeping Act (legitimate interest / legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).

Retention

Member information is retained for the duration of membership and deleted at the latest 3 years after withdrawal — unless the Bookkeeping Act requires longer retention (5 years for accounting records). Enquiries via the contact form are generally deleted within one year.

Disclosure

DIF Aarhus does not disclose your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is shared only:

  • with the association's bank in connection with membership payments,
  • with magazine publishers (Bladet Israel, Magbit-Nyt) solely to deliver to you the magazines that membership entitles you to,
  • where legislation requires us to (tax authorities, public bodies, etc.).

This website

This website (www.dif-aarhus.dk) is static and uses no tracking cookies, no behavioural content, and no third-party analytics such as Google Analytics. The only thing we set in your browser is a local preference (localStorage) to remember your light/dark theme choice. This setting is not shared with any third party.

Fonts (Inter, Cormorant Garamond, Source Serif 4, Frank Ruhl Libre) are loaded via Google Fonts. In doing so Google receives your IP address. We have no influence on Google's subsequent processing. See Google's privacy policy.

External links

The website contains many links to external sites. When you click an external link you leave DIF Aarhus's website and become subject to the respective site's own privacy policy. DIF Aarhus is not responsible for content on external sites.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Access: to know what information we hold on you.
  • Rectification: to have incorrect information corrected.
  • Erasure: to have your information deleted (where legislation does not require continued retention).
  • Restriction: to restrict processing in certain cases.
  • Objection: to object to the processing.
  • Portability: to receive your data in a common format.
  • Withdrawal of consent: you can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

Email dif_webmaster@yahoo.dk if you wish to exercise any of these rights.

Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency regarding our processing of your personal data. See www.datatilsynet.dk.

Changes

This policy may change. The current version is always available on this page.
