Principle 4 · Humanitarian support

Support humanitarian causes in Israel.

One of DIF Aarhus's four guiding principles is to support humanitarian initiatives for the benefit of Israel's population. Below are some of the organisations whose work the association recommends — all independent of DIF, all with transparent accounts.

Emergency aid · the Red Star of David

Magen David Adom (MDA)

Israel's Red Cross. The country-wide ambulance service, pre-hospital emergency care and blood bank. MDA runs thousands of volunteers and saves lives regardless of whether the patient is Jewish, Muslim, Christian, soldier, civilian or tourist. Recognised by the International Red Cross movement in 2006.

mdais.org →
Israel's development · since 1920

Keren Hayesod — United Israel Appeal

Israel's primary fundraising organisation since 1920. Builds homes for new immigrants, funds youth movements, helps Holocaust survivors and supports peripheral communities. The Danish branch is known as Den Danske Israelindsamling (DDI) and publishes the magazine Magbit-Nyt, which DIF members receive.

kerenhayesod.dk →
Environment · since 1901

KKL — Keren Kayemeth LeIsrael

Israel's oldest environmental organisation. Famous for having planted more than 240 million trees and developed the green areas of the desert. Manages water resources, parks and ecological restoration. "Plant a tree in Israel" is their classic gift programme.

kkl-jnf.org →
Children & families

WIZO — Women's International Zionist Organization

Worldwide women's organisation supporting children, women and families in Israel — daycare centres, crisis shelters, schools, integration programmes for newcomers. WIZO Denmark has a long and active tradition.

wizo.org →
Holocaust memorial · research

Yad Vashem

The world's central institution for Holocaust remembrance and research. An archive, a museum, an education centre, a names database and a memorial landscape on Mount Herzl in Jerusalem. Protects and educates — so that it never happens again.

yadvashem.org →
Aliyah & integration

The Jewish Agency for Israel

Helps Jews from around the world make aliyah — move to Israel — and supports integration, language teaching and families in crisis. Active since 1929.

jewishagency.org →

Note: DIF Aarhus does not itself receive donations or fundraise for external causes — but the association recommends the above organisations as well-established, independent and transparent. Each has its own accounts and boards. Read their annual reports before donating.
