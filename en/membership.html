Become a member

You are welcome at DIF.

Anyone — Jewish or non-Jewish — who adheres to the purpose and program of the Danish-Israeli Association can become a member, provided they are not a member of another organisation whose aims contradict ours.

Per person

175 DKK/year

A full year's membership — including magazines and member discount on the lecture series.

Per couple

225 DKK/year

For two people at the same address.

Young 18–25

95 DKK/year

Student rate for those who want to take part.

What you receive as a member

  • Access to the lecture series at member price (350 DKK) instead of 450 DKK.
  • Invitations to extra lectures, wine tastings, concerts and other events — including those not advertised broadly.
  • Bladet Israel three times a year (the Danish Zionist Federation and the friendship associations).
  • Magbit-Nyt three times a year (Keren Hayesod / Den Danske Israelindsamling).
  • Member newsletters and emails about upcoming activities.
  • Voice and vote at the annual general meeting; the right to stand for the board.
  • Eligibility for DIF's trips to Israel.

How to join

  1. Bank transfer using the details to the right. Include name, address and email in the message.
  2. Email us with your name, address and contact details — we'll send the rest.
  3. Hand a slip with your details to a board member at one of our events.
  4. Pay the treasurer directly at one of our events.

If you join at the same time as you sign up for the lecture series, you of course pay the member price for the series. Transfer the combined amount and let us know which is which.
